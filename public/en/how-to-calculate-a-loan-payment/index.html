How to calculate a loan payment

A typical installment loan payment depends on how much you borrow, the interest rate, and how long you repay. The loan calculator on Free Online Calculator can estimate a payment from those inputs. The result is an illustration, not an offer from a bank.

Steps

  1. Gather the three main inputs. You need the loan amount (principal), the annual interest rate the lender quotes, and the number of payments (for example monthly payments over a number of years).
  2. Enter them in the loan calculator. Open the loan calculator and fill in principal, rate, and term. Use the same payment frequency the lender uses, usually monthly.
  3. Read the payment and the total interest. The tool should show an estimated payment. Look at the total you would repay as well, not only the monthly figure.
  4. Compare with the lender's quote. Banks may add fees, insurance, or a different compounding method. Treat the calculator as a check, then read the official repayment schedule.

What this estimate leaves out

Taxes, compulsory insurance, arrangement fees, and variable rates can change the real payment. A mortgage or micro-loan in your country may also follow a required formula that differs from a generic amortization.

Do not use this as financial advice

The calculator is a free arithmetic aid. For a decision that affects your budget, use the lender's figures or a qualified adviser.
